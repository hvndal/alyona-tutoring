How a lesson works

i.

Get to know you

Every lesson is personalized. We start by finding where you feel confident and where you need more support.

ii.

Explain it clearly

We work through concepts together with clear explanations, examples and colour-coded notes.

iii.

Practice, practice

Students understand a concept far more deeply once they apply it. Step by step, building toward harder questions.

iv.

Go it alone

Ask questions, learn from mistakes, and build the confidence to tackle similar problems independently.
